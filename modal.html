Important Notice: Miro Licensing & Cost Awareness

Miro License Management

Every action counts — please use Miro access responsibly.

In Miro, access is granted instantly the moment an invited user begins interacting with a board. Even a single comment can automatically trigger a paid license allocation — with immediate cost impact to Hexagon.

💶

Each activated Miro user costs Hexagon $165 per year.
Every editing or commenting action immediately activates a paid license.

Before Inviting Someone to Miro

Please ensure the person you are inviting meets both of the following criteria:

  • They truly require editing or commenting access — not just view-only.
  • They will actively and regularly use Miro for their work.

If You Were Invited But Don't Need Edit Access

If view-only access is sufficient for your needs, please be aware of the following:

  • Do not add comments to any board.
  • Do not start editing any board content.
  • Any editing or commenting action immediately activates a paid license.
📋 What to do if you only need view access? Contact the board owner and confirm that view-only access is sufficient. Do not interact with the board beyond reading its content.

Why This Matters

Unintended license activations accumulate into significant unplanned costs. By being intentional about how we grant and use Miro access, we protect Hexagon's software budget and ensure licenses remain available for those who genuinely need them.

🎯 Let's keep our Miro licenses lean, intentional, and under control.
